# Muvi Lite

**Your Streaming Platform. Your Brand. Your Server.**

Muvi Lite is a free, self-hosted streaming platform developed by Avinance, designed to help
developers, startups, businesses, and organizations launch their own branded streaming services.

Built for lightweight deployment on a VPS or cloud server, Muvi Lite brings together the essential
tools for managing and delivering digital media.

## Features

- Video on Demand (VOD)
- Movies and TV series
- Short-form video content
- Music and audio content
- Advertising support
- Content management
- Custom branding
- Self-hosted deployment
- Extensible platform architecture
- Integration with Avinance Player

## Free for Everyone

Muvi Lite is designed for unrestricted personal and commercial use.

You can build and operate your own streaming service, monetize your content, and customize the
open-source platform without software licensing fees or artificial limits on users, content, or
revenue.

Actual capacity depends on your infrastructure.
